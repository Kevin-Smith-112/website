Our Story

A grocery store rooted in Lake Country

Lake Country sits between Kalamalka and Wood Lake, in the heart of some of the Okanagan's oldest and most productive orchard land. LCLP was started to give that produce a proper front door — a single place in town where neighbours can find the week's fruit and vegetable harvest from the farms just up the road.

🚜

Grown Down the Road

Everything on our shelves comes from Lake Country growers, most within a ten-minute drive of the store. We know the families behind every bin.

🌤️

Picked at Peak

Because our growers are so close, produce moves from orchard to store the same day it's picked — no long cold-storage supply chain required.

🤝

Fair to Farmers

We pay our growers a fair price for their harvest so the orchards and farms that make Lake Country special can keep farming for generations to come.
